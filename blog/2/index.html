A Rouge OpenAI Model Hacked Medicare

When It Happened


A OpenAI model in the training phase breached the Services Australia Medicare Statistics portal on June 18.
It took until August for OpenAI to detect the breach. OpenAI took their time in the IR phase and alerted Services Australia on September 10


Are You Affected

No data was exfiltrated from the breach.
