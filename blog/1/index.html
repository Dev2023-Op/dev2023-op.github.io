FBI Employee Data Breach

When It Happened


ShinyHunters exfiltrated data from fbijobs.gov on Monday 21 September
ShinyHunters replaced the homepage of apply.fbijobs.gov with a seizure notice.


Are You Affected


Past and current workers and their family's are affected.
FBI is working with law enforcement to detain the group responsible.
