Why I love VDP


As a begginer to cybersecurity and not wanting to pay for subscritions to services like tryhackme and hackthebox, vulnerability disclosure programs are one of the only places that i can safely practice my skills.

A lot of vulnerability disclosure programms also offer: - Money - Recognition in their hall of fame

It is also important to practice your hacking skills if you want to grow your skillset.

How i find programs

To find decent programs i paste the following into google: plaintext vulnerability "disclosure" responsible

This google dork finds vulnerability disclodures and responsible disclosures (same thing with different names)

What i look for in a program

I prefer to engage in programs that offer "safe harbour" witch means their not going to sue you. I also make sure that they are okay with with me testing the vulnerability.

What not to do in a VDP

  • DoS attacks (denial of service) unless they say you can
  • Report the vulnerability multiple times
