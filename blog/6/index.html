Why I switched to AFFiNE for my notes


Ease of import

AFFiNE allows for .md, pdf and images to be imported. This allows obsidian notes to be imported.

Price

Self hosting AFFiNE comes at no cost as well as basic cloud sync. There are paid plans you can use.

Self hostability

Self hosting a AFFiNE server is low difficulty, low maintnance, low resource consumption and allows for access controll and other cool features.

The setup is fairly intuitive and only requires 5 steps when installing with docker compose.
