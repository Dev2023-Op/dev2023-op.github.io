Introduction To Cybersecurity


What is cybersecurity


Cybersecurity is the procces and practice of securing digital information and systems to lessen the risk of attacks, leaks and breaches.


Why does cybersecurity matter


Securing digital systems is essential for:

  • Preventing leakage of critical data
  • Ensuring all information is authentic
  • Ensuring critical infrastructure is in safe hands

Jobs in cybersecurity


SOC(Security Operations Center) Analyst


A SOC analyst monitors logs and security notifications to spot potential attacks and data exfiltration.
SOC roles are split into two categories. The first one being SOC analyst lv1, this role triages alerts on a SOC dashboard that informs them of intormation such as:

  • Possible attacks
  • Possible attack data
  • Event severity

Aswell as SOC analyst lv2, this role verifies and acts uppon alerts that have been flagged by SOC analyst lv1


Digital Forensics and Incident Response


Digital Forensics and Incident Response(commonly refered to as DFIR) investigates information from a known attack to determine the source of the attack, what the attack was, the timeline of the attack and what information and systems are compromised. DFIR roles work with law enforcement to pinpoint the attacker and take resonable actions to ensure that the attack does not occur again.


Penetration Tester


A penetration tester simulates realworld attacks and attack methods on systems(with permission) to find vulnerabilitys in systems in order to have those vulnerabilitys patched so they cannot be exploited in real world attacks.
