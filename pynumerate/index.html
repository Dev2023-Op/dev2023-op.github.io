What is pynumerate


pynumerate is a web application OSINT tool that executes modules to gather intelligence on a target website.

pynumerate is made for cybersecurity proffesionals who legaly simulate a real world attack on their client or company.
This helps the cybersecurity and development team identify vulnerabilitys in their system before they are exploited by bad actors
